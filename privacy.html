WitzBase · Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Your channels. Your content. A clear explanation of your data.

Who maintains WitzBase

WitzBase is a privately maintained desktop application for managing the maintainer’s own media channels and channels whose owners explicitly authorize access. The maintainer uses the GitHub account Ilia-1103. For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact bulbulator7766@gmail.com. WitzBase is not a Google or YouTube product.

What the application accesses

With your permission, WitzBase uses YouTube API Services, including YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. It accesses channel identifiers, names, profile images and banners; video identifiers and metadata; aggregate channel and video analytics such as views, watch time, subscriber totals and changes, engagement, traffic sources, device and geographic breakdowns when available. Revenue reports are requested only if you separately grant the optional monetary analytics permission. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts or Google Drive.

Videos, content and local records

The application handles video files, images, music and text you select, plus titles, descriptions, publication times, templates and queue records. Uploading sends the selected video and publication metadata to YouTube. Data in the local content library is separate from data returned by Google. WitzBase does not require your Google password; Google handles account sign in.

Why data is used

Channel information identifies the selected channel. Analytics reports and local snapshots support reporting and comparisons over time. Selected files and metadata are used to prepare videos, upload them and schedule publication. Queue and error records help show progress and troubleshoot failures. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, used for credit decisions or used to train general purpose AI models.

Where information is stored

The desktop application stores its library, reports, snapshots and work history on the computer where it runs. OAuth access and refresh tokens and client configuration are protected using Windows data protection. API traffic is sent to Google over HTTPS. There is no WitzBase hosted account or central analytics server. Anyone with sufficient access to your computer or backups may be able to access local records. The public website does not receive your channel credentials, videos or analytics.

Sharing and service providers

Google receives API requests, authorized account information, uploads and the associated metadata needed for the requested features. Data is not otherwise sent to third parties by these channel management features. The maintainer may receive information you voluntarily include in support email; avoid sending tokens or passwords. See the Google Privacy Policy. WitzBase’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The current desktop version retains reports, historical snapshots, publication records and local backups until they are removed; it does not yet apply an automatic expiration period to all stored API records. Disconnecting a channel deletes its saved local authorization but does not automatically erase its history, selected media or backups. To remove local records, contact the maintainer for help deleting the relevant channel records and backup copies on the computer where the application is installed. Support requests are retained only as needed to respond and resolve the request. Deleting local records does not delete videos or data held by YouTube.

Your choices and revoking access

You can stop using the application and disconnect a channel in its API connection settings. You can also revoke WitzBase’s access at Google account permissions. Revocation prevents further authorized API use but does not itself erase previously saved local copies. Request deletion using bulbulator7766@gmail.com; the maintainer will help locate and remove the applicable records and backups. Where applicable, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by the maintainer.

This public website

This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no login form, advertising, embedded videos, analytics script, external fonts or application cookies. Your browser sends normal request information, including your IP address, to the hosting provider. GitHub may process hosting and security logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your chosen email service.

Changes and contact

Changes will be published here with an updated date. Material changes to the use of Google user data require notice and, where required, renewed consent before the new use. Privacy questions: bulbulator7766@gmail.com.
