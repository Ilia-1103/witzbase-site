How the YouTube connection works

01

Choose a channel

Connect an account in the desktop application. Google opens its own permission screen.

02

Approve the access

Allow channel information, analytics and uploads. Revenue reports require a separate optional permission.

03

Stay in control

Review publication settings and revoke the application’s access from your Google account when needed.
